1. Synopsis 
QuickBite is a full-stack Online Food Delivery Platform inspired by Zomato and Swiggy. It connects 
customers, restaurants, and delivery agents on a single unified platform. Customers can discover 
nearby restaurants, browse category-wise menus, build a cart, place orders, track deliveries in real 
time, and rate their experience. Restaurant owners can register their establishments, manage menus, 
toggle item availability, and track incoming orders and earnings. Delivery agents can register, view 
assigned orders, update live location, and mark deliveries complete. 
 
The platform supports four primary roles: Guests (unauthenticated visitors), Customers, Restaurant 
Owners, and Delivery Agents — each with a purpose-built dashboard — along with a Platform 
Administrator who oversees the entire ecosystem. QuickBite is architected as a microservices system 
modelled on the EShoppingZone reference pattern, with independent services for authentication/user 
management, restaurant profiles, menu management, cart management, order lifecycle, 
payment/wallet, delivery agent management, reviews/ratings, and notifications — all integrated through 
a Spring MVC website controller layer. 
 
2. Detailed Requirements 
2.1 General Requirements 
•​
Guests can browse restaurants, view menus, and search without logging in. 
•​
Users must register or log in (email/password or OAuth) to place orders or manage profiles. 
•​
Role-based access control: Customer, Restaurant Owner, Delivery Agent, and Admin. 
•​
All users can update their profile, contact information, and password. 
•​
In-app, email, and SMS notifications are dispatched for all key order lifecycle events. 
•​
Admin panel provides full platform management, restaurant approval, agent verification, and 
analytics. 
 
2.2 Customer Requirements 
•​
Register and log in via email/password or Google/GitHub OAuth. 
•​
Browse nearby restaurants filtered by location, cuisine, rating, delivery time, or cost-for-two. 
•​
View detailed restaurant profiles including menu categories, item descriptions, prices, and 
ratings. 
•​
Add items to cart from a single restaurant; prompted to clear cart when switching restaurants. 
•​
Update item quantities in cart; remove items; apply promo codes for discounts. 
•​
Place orders with Cash on Delivery (COD) or online payment (wallet, card, UPI). 
•​
Track order status in real-time: Placed → Confirmed → Preparing → Picked Up → Delivered. 
•​
View complete order history; reorder from any past order in one click. 
•​
Cancel orders within an allowed time window before preparation begins. 
•​
Rate restaurants (food quality) and delivery agents (delivery experience) after order completion.

2.6 Payment & Wallet Requirements 
•​
Customers can pay via COD, debit/credit card, UPI, or wallet balance. 
•​
Wallet supports deposit (add money) and debit (payment) operations; each generates a 
statement record. 
•​
Wallet balance is validated before wallet payment is accepted to prevent negative balance. 
•​
Refunds are credited back to the original payment mode or to the wallet within 3-5 business 
days. 
•​
Payment records store amount, mode, transaction ID, status, and timestamps for full audit. 
 
2.7 Notification Requirements 
•​
Notifications dispatched for: order placed, order confirmed by restaurant, food being prepared, 
picked up by agent, delivered. 
•​
Each notification carries recipientId, type, relatedId (orderId), and a deep-link URL. 
•​
Multi-channel: in-app notification centre, email (JavaMailSender), and SMS (Twilio/AWS SNS). 
•​
Restaurant owners receive audio + in-app alert for every new incoming order. 
•​
Unread badge count displayed in real time in the navigation bar. 
•​
Admin can broadcast platform-wide promotional or informational notifications.

4. Class Diagrams — All Services 
QuickBite follows the same layered microservices architecture as the EShoppingZone reference 
system. Each service comprises five layers: Entity/POJO (domain model), Repository Interface (data 
access), Service Interface (business contract), ServiceImpl (business logic), and REST Resource (API 
exposure). The eleven class diagrams below detail every service in the platform.

4.1 Auth / User-Service 
The Auth/User-Service is the security gateway for the entire platform. It manages user registration, 
login, JWT token lifecycle, OAuth2 provider integration (Google/GitHub), profile management, 
password changes, and account deactivation. Every protected endpoint on the platform verifies the 
JWT through this service, and user identity resolution for all roles (Customer, Restaurant Owner, 
Delivery Agent, Admin) is centralised here.

4.3 Menu-Service

MenuCategories (e.g., Starters, Main Course, Desserts), each containing a list of MenuItems. Items 
carry nutritional metadata (calories, tags), a discounted price field, and an availability toggle so owners 
can instantly mark items as sold out without deleting them. The service supports veg/non-veg filtering 
and keyword search across menu items.

4.4 Cart-Service 
The Cart-Service manages a single active cart per customer. Each Cart is associated with one 
restaurant (to enforce single-restaurant ordering) and contains a list of CartItems, each capturing the 
menu item reference, price snapshot, quantity, and customisation notes. The service validates that all 
items belong to the cart's restaurant, computes the total, and supports promo code application via 
inter-service validation.

4.5 Order-Service 
The Order-Service is the central orchestration service of the platform. It converts a confirmed cart into 
an Order, records OrderItems as a snapshot, and manages the complete order lifecycle through status 
transitions. On placement, it calls the Cart-Service to snapshot items, the Payment-Service to process 
payment, and the Schedule-Service to assign a delivery agent. It supports order cancellation with 
refund triggering and reorder functionality.

4.6 Payment / Wallet-Service 
The Payment/Wallet-Service processes order payments and manages customer e-wallets. Each 
Payment record is linked to exactly one order and stores the amount, payment mode, transaction ID, 
currency, and status. The Wallet entity tracks a customer's running balance with a linked list of 
WalletStatements recording deposits and debits. The service validates sufficient balance before wallet 
payments and triggers refunds on order cancellation.

4.8 Review / Rating-Service 
The Review/Rating-Service allows customers to submit dual ratings after order completion — a food 
quality rating for the restaurant and a delivery experience rating for the agent. One review is permitted 
per order, enforced by a unique constraint on orderId. Average ratings are computed and pushed back 
to the Restaurant-Service and Delivery-Agent-Service to update their respective avgRating fields, which 
surface on discovery cards.

pings. The service tracks read/unread state, exposes an unread badge count, and supports bulk

5. Microservices Architecture Overview 
QuickBite is structured as a set of independently deployable microservices following the 
EShoppingZone reference pattern. Each service owns its own database schema, REST API contract, 
service interface, and business logic. Synchronous inter-service calls use RestTemplate; asynchronous 
order-event notifications are routed via RabbitMQ.

Passwords stored as bcrypt hashes; JWT tokens expire in 24 hours;

8. Glossary 
Term 
Definition 
Customer 
A registered user who browses restaurants, places food orders, and 
provides reviews on QuickBite 
Restaurant Owner 
A merchant who registers their restaurant, manages the digital menu, and 
processes incoming customer orders 
Delivery Agent 
A registered rider who accepts pickup and delivery assignments and 
updates live location during delivery 
Order Lifecycle 
The sequence of states an order passes through: Placed → Confirmed → 
Preparing → Picked Up → Delivered 
Cart 
A temporary session storing a customer's selected menu items from a 
single restaurant prior to checkout 
CartItem 
A single menu item entry in the cart capturing quantity, price snapshot, and 
customisation notes 
OrderItem 
An immutable snapshot of a CartItem saved to the Order on placement for 
historical accuracy 
Wallet 
A pre-loaded digital balance linked to a customer account used for 
cashless order payments 
Promo Code 
A discount voucher applied at cart level to reduce the final order total 
Delivery Radius 
The maximum distance in kilometres from a restaurant within which it 
accepts delivery orders 
Geo-proximity 
Location-based restaurant and agent discovery using Haversine distance 
calculation on GPS coordinates 
Food Rating 
A 1–5 star score given by the customer rating the quality of food from a 
restaurant 
Delivery Rating 
A 1–5 star score given by the customer rating the professionalism and 
speed of the delivery agent 
Approval 
The admin action required to activate a newly registered restaurant before 
it becomes visible to customers 
Verification 
The admin action required to activate a delivery agent after reviewing their 
identity and vehicle documents 
COD 
Cash on Delivery — a payment mode where the customer pays cash 
directly to the delivery agent on receipt
